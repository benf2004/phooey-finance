# phooey! finance launch playbook

## Offer and audience

Start with US freelancers who use Google Sheets to reconcile client income, business expenses, and quarterly tax payments. The paid product includes the dashboard and one connected workbook for $29 per year. Optional SimpleFIN bank feeds cost $15 per year paid directly to SimpleFIN. A connected customer spends $44 before applicable tax. Phooey receives $29. Check the providers' pages before updating comparison copy. [Tiller pricing](https://tiller.com/pricing/) · [SimpleFIN pricing](https://beta-bridge.simplefin.org/)

The first commercial milestone is five people who pay, import their own data, save a ledger edit from Sheets, and complete a quarterly review. Only collect details a participant agrees to share. Use fictional books for public demos.

## Founder-led campaign

| Timing | Work | Evidence for the next decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weeks 1–2 | Recruit 15 US freelancers through opt-in introductions and communities allowing research. Ask to watch the last month-end close. Invite 10 design partners to try the free workbook and show the actual $29/$44 pricing. | Five people willing to join a paid beta. Record the objections from people who decline. |
| Weeks 3–8 | Observe setup, Tiller preview, the first feed refresh, and the first split. Document where each person stops. Fix the most frequent blocker and watch the next participant attempt it unaided. | Five participants reach an accurate first estimate without founder intervention. |
| Weeks 9–12 | Open a 25-customer paid beta. Publish the migration guide, workbook download, and real product demo. Give participating bookkeepers a workbook they can show clients. | 80% activate by day seven. Of those eligible for the 30-day cohort, 60% return within 30 days. |
| Public launch | Publish the approved Marketplace listing and public pages. Ask active customers for permission to quote their experiences. Expand the best converting channel toward 100 subscribers. | Positive first-year contribution after payment fees, infrastructure, refunds, acquisition, and support time. |

### Interview guide

1. Walk me through the last time you closed a month or prepared a quarterly payment. Where did the numbers come from?
2. Which steps did you repeat? Which number did you double-check outside the workbook?
3. Show how you handle a purchase with business and personal portions.
4. Try the example workbook. Without coaching, explain how you would connect a bank and what you would pay annually.
5. Would you sign up at this price for your next close? Record the specific reason for the answer.

### Publishable assets

- The public site includes Home, Google Sheets, Pricing, Compare Tiller, and Free Workbook. The comparison links to a browser-only file preview.
- Show a bank transaction, the two parts of a split, and the changed estimate. Label every sample-data capture. The free workbook's reserve calculation uses the customer's chosen percentage. The connected workbook uses the ledger tax calculation.
- For the Marketplace listing use **phooey! finance for Google Sheets**. The short description is “Manage freelance transactions, split expenses, and review quarterly estimates in your connected phooey! finance workbook.” Link to the product, privacy, terms, and support pages.
- Use the waitlist only for beta invitations. Save the participant's permission before publishing a quote, screenshot, or case study.

### Economics and measurement

At 25, 100, and 1,000 active subscribers gross annual recurring revenue is $725, $2,900, and $29,000. Calculate contribution per customer from actual Stripe payouts, refunds, paid infrastructure, and support minutes multiplied by the founder's hourly cost. Track payments to SimpleFIN outside Phooey revenue. Begin with organic distribution and a proposed $200 total experiment ceiling, subject to the founder's spending decision.

Run the cohort report against the commercial control database. Activation requires a completed CSV/bank import and a successful spreadsheet edit. The 30-day return metric counts visits or sidebar activity on a later day. Ask the first 25 customers which page, person, or template brought them in. Record referral source, support minutes, and acquisition spend in the launch ledger without financial account details. Review monthly. [Cohort queries](../hosted/reports.sql)
