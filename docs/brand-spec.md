# phooey! finance

Use lowercase **phooey! finance** for the product and **phooey!** for the standalone wordmark. The drawn lettering is based on Bricolage Grotesque. Keep the exclamation mark with the name. The public asset directory contains outlined SVGs for light and dark surfaces, the standalone wordmark, and the exclamation favicon.

Use dark green ink (#173C35), warm paper (#FFFEF8), and a rust accent (#BD522D). Report charts use green for income and terracotta for expenses, with labeled totals and a numeric table. Product screenshots must use example records.

Foundation is the starting workbook. Spending Trends, Transactions, Categories, AutoCat, Monthly Budget, Yearly Budget, Accounts, Balances and Balance History share the same inputs. Transaction Tracker, Business Dashboard, Quarterly Earnings and the two tax views extend it. Quarterly Earnings uses calendar quarters. The tax views use the January–March, April–May, June–August and September–December payment periods.
