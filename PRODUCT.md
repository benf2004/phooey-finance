# Product

phooey! finance and phooey! invoicer

## Platform
adaptive

## Stack
React and TypeScript on Cloudflare Workers with D1. The iOS app uses SwiftUI, Swift Charts and PDFKit for its overview, transaction register and invoice editor. It connects to the hosted workspace API after system-browser authentication.

## Users
Solopreneurs who invoice clients, categorize transactions, and review business performance themselves.

## Product Purpose
Answer how the business is doing for a selected period and turn completed work into an invoice.

## Positioning
A $29 annual hosted workspace for bookkeeping and invoicing. Self-hosted editions are available under AGPL-3.0-only. Optional bank syncing is a separate service.

## Operating Context
Review the business on a desktop or phone. Draft privately, then issue and share an invoice deliberately. Manual entry and CSV import work without a bank subscription.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Respect each workspace’s data boundary, separate currencies, invoice revisions, and the difference between drafts and issued documents. Keep the existing single-business self-hosted products usable.

## Brand Commitments
Preserve the lowercase phooey! finance wordmark, warm paper, dark green ink, and rust accent specified in docs/brand-spec.md. The public site names the solopreneur audience.

## Evidence on Hand
The owner selected business review as the first dashboard task. Invoice Generator is the requested reference for an editor in document order. The existing ledger and invoicer define the supported actions.

## Product Principles
Let owners trace amounts to records, see the effect of invoice edits, and understand which actions make a draft available to a client.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Label editable fields and financial amounts explicitly, preserve keyboard operation, and provide numeric values alongside charts. Support narrow screens, visible focus, and reduced motion.
