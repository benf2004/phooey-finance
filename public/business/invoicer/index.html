Questions, answered.

How much does it cost?

The hosted Finance and Invoicer bundle costs $29 per year after a 14-day free trial. A card is required; cancel before the trial ends to avoid the annual charge.

Can I use more than one business?

Yes. Add a business from the sidebar and switch between them without signing out.

Can clients pay through Invoicer?

Invoicer creates invoices and records payments you’ve received. It does not process payments. Add your preferred payment instructions to the invoice.

How do I connect an AI assistant?

Open API & MCP in the app, create a key, and copy the MCP server URL into a client that supports Bearer authentication. Each key is limited to one business.

Can I self-host?

Yes. Get the source and Cloudflare setup instructions on GitHub.
