Privacy and data controls

Phooey stores your ledger to provide the dashboard, bank import, invoicing, exports, and recovery.

Where information goes

Each hosted workspace has a dedicated ledger database, file bucket, and job queue. Hosted Invoicer uses a separate database for invoices, customer details, and logos. Cloudflare hosts the service. Your identity provider verifies your account. Stripe handles card payments. SimpleFIN provides the bank accounts you select.

Anyone with an active invoice share link can view that invoice and download its PDF. Revoke the link from the invoice actions to end access.

Activity, retention and deletion

Phooey records signup, workspace visits, and imported-data availability as dated events for operating the service. These events exclude transaction descriptions, balances, and institution names. Financial records remain in the workspace until removed. Request account deletion or removal from the beta list through support. Billing records and provider backups follow their applicable retention requirements.
