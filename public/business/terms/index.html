Subscription terms

The hosted service is intended for individual US freelancers. The customer manages access to their shared invoice links and optional bank connection.

Payment and renewal

New subscribers receive a 14-day free trial starting at checkout. A card is required. Cancel in Manage billing before the trial ends to avoid the first charge. After the trial, the subscription costs US $29 per year before applicable taxes. Stripe processes annual renewals. Cancel from Manage billing before renewal. Optional SimpleFIN service is billed separately and can be cancelled through SimpleFIN.

For a refund request, contact support with your billing email and transaction date. Support reviews requests individually. If an approved refund ends access early, support will explain the export window.

Data and estimates

After your paid term, CSV and JSON export remain available for 30 days. Download receipt files separately before expiry. Download invoice PDFs before expiry. Institution coverage and transaction history vary by provider. Review estimates and tax assumptions before relying on them for a payment or filing.
