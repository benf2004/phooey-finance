# phooey! finance for solopreneurs

## Users
A person who manages client work, bills for it, and reviews the books without an accounting team. The first dashboard task is reviewing income, expenses, and net income over a selected period. A return visit to Invoicer may start with an unfinished draft or a payment against an issued invoice.

## Product
The hosted Finance and Invoicer bundle costs $29 per year. The iPhone client opens the same workspace. Self-hosted deployments use an admin key. The existing bank connector uses SimpleFIN Bridge and has separate billing.

## Constraints
Preserve the phooey! finance wordmark, customer data, invoice PDF and share-link behavior, and currency boundaries. Keep the hosted workspace isolated per subscriber. Both editions distribute their application source under AGPL-3.0-only.

## Ethical stance
Show recurring cost before checkout. Ask before issuing an invoice. Explain who can open a share link. Use fictional records for public previews. Show missing amounts as unavailable while data is loading.

## Success
A business owner can identify income, expenses, and net income for a chosen period, follow an amount to its records, and edit an invoice in its reading order before issuing it.
